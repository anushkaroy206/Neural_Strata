WHY THIS MODEL EXISTS
  Model 2 extrapolates displacement and cannot tell WHAT is moving the
  ground. A truck reads as a short TTF and fires the siren; a crack masked
  by background vibration reads as stable. M1 supplies the missing
  question — what kind of event is this — and may never suppress an alert.

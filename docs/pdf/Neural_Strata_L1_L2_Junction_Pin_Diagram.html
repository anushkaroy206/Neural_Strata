L1 / L2 → Junctions, LED strips and buzzers

Every wire, every pin, for the two level hubs. Nothing here needs a computer at the junction.

First, the thing that trips everyone up: a junction has no sensor and no microcontroller. J1, J2, J3, J4 are just places in the mine where tunnels meet — and the only thing sitting there is an 8-LED strip. The strip is dumb; the level hub is what drives it. So there are no "sensor wires to the junction" — there is one data wire from the hub to each strip, plus power from the rail.

The five rules that cover all of it

1 · Only blue goes to a GPIO

Strip DIN is the only strip wire that touches the hub. Red and black come from the LED rail.

2 · 330 Ω inline, hub end

One per data run, soldered near the hub, not at the strip.

3 · 1N4148 on every red

Drops the strip to ~4.4 V so 3.3 V logic reads as a clean HIGH.

4 · 1000 µF at every strip

Across VDD and GND, at the strip. Stops the first-LED flicker.

5 · Arrow = data flow

The printed arrow shows which way data travels. DIN is the pad at its tail, DOUT at its head.

Pin numbers repeat, jobs don't

21/22 are strips on a hub, MPU-6050 on a node. Always read the board in your hand.
